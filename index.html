GOOD TO KNOW

Simple answers before you book.

Do I need to provide cleaning products?

No. Standard cleaning products and equipment are brought to the job. If you prefer specific products to be used in your home, that’s absolutely fine too.

Do you offer deep cleaning?

Light one-off resets can be quoted, but specialist deep-cleaning work is not part of the launch service.

Do you clean commercial kitchens?

Only basic surfaces, floors, bins and front-of-house areas. Specialist extraction, grease and industrial kitchen cleaning is not currently offered.

Which areas do you cover?

Launch coverage is Leatherhead, Ashtead and Epsom, with nearby areas considered depending on the booking.
